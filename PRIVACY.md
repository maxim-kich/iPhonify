# Privacy

iPhonify uses trackpad contact positions to recognize gestures. Accessibility identifies the focused fullscreen window and lets the app suppress scrolling during a gesture. Screen Recording creates the temporary images used for the transition.

Screen images and blurred wallpaper stay in memory. Normal operation does not save recordings or screenshots. iPhonify includes no analytics or telemetry.

Diagnostic text is stored in `iPhonify.log` in your temporary directory. It may contain app identifiers, window and Space identifiers, timings, and error codes. It does not contain captured images and is not uploaded automatically. Review logs before sharing them.

Checking for updates contacts GitHub. Downloading an update and restarting require separate confirmation. Automatic checks, silent installation, cookies, and system profiling are disabled. The updater does not send screenshots or logs.

Creator and license links open their respective websites. Those services receive normal connection information and have their own privacy policies.

To revoke access, quit iPhonify and disable its Accessibility and Screen Recording access in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
